This is the site for my famous cheesecake recipe.

Crust:

210g Flour

70g Sugar

140g vegan Butter (soft)

Combine everything and rest in the fridge for 30 minutes

Batter:

500g vegan Skyr

120g vegan Cream Cheese

200ml vegan Cream

120g vegan Butter (melted)

135g Sugar

45g Starch

1 tabelspoon of Vanilla Extract

Zest and Juice of half a lemon

Combine everything and Rest in the fridge whilst putting the crust into a pan.

Pouring the batter into the crust and putting in the oven for 1 hour at 200°C.

In the meanwhile prepare the Raspberry Sauce:

300g Raspberries

2 Spoons Sugar

2 Spoons Starch

Letting simmer in a pan until it reaches a slightly thick consistency.

Taking the pan out of the oven and letting it cool, after: putting the Raspberry sauce on top.
